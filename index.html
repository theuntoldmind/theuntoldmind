Behind The Untold Mind

Hi, I'm Natalie Stello.

I've spent fourteen years on the administrative side of healthcare. I've watched capable, intelligent people lose hours, money, and care because nobody explained how the system works or what the paperwork actually said.

The Untold Mind is where I translate it: the human stories that help us understand each other, and the plain-language tools that help you speak up for yourself and the people you care for.

Questions

Before you buy

Who are the guides for?

Adults managing their own care or supporting a child, parent, or partner, especially anyone who has felt lost in insurance calls, bills, referrals, or medical terminology.

What format are they in?

Digital PDFs delivered by email right after purchase. Read them on your phone, tablet, or computer, or print them.

Is this medical or legal advice?

No. Everything here is educational. It helps you understand the system and prepare better questions; always confirm decisions with your providers, your insurer, or a qualified professional.

Should I get the bundle or one book?

Start with The Patient's Playbook if you need a plan. Start with Medical Speak, Decoded if the language is what trips you up. The bundle gives you both for $29.99, plus the printable checklist.
